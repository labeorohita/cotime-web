Privacy Policy

Dovabl

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

Dovabl is a pre-release software application currently available for testing purposes. The app is operated by an independent developer. Features and functionality may change during testing and development.

For privacy-related inquiries or support, please contact:
contact@dovabl.com

1. Information We Collect

We may collect and process the following information:

  • Email address (used for authentication)
  • Profile information (name, photo, city, interests, bio)
  • Availability data (activities created, joined, or interacted with)
  • Participation and feedback data
  • Safety and moderation data (blocks, reports)
  • Location data (if you enable location permissions)

We do not collect payment information.

2. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

We process personal data based on:

  • Your consent (e.g., enabling location access)
  • Performance of a contract (providing and testing the Dovabl service)
  • Legitimate interests (platform safety, fraud prevention, abuse prevention)

3. How We Use Information

We use your information to:

  • Enable activity discovery and matching
  • Display profile and availability information to other users
  • Improve recommendations
  • Maintain safety and enforce rules
  • Prevent abuse, spam, or fraudulent behavior
  • Evaluate performance and improve the app during testing

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data.

Your profile and activity information may be visible to other users depending on your visibility settings.

We use third-party infrastructure providers (such as Supabase and hosting providers) to securely store and process data. These providers may process data on our behalf.

5. Location Data

If enabled, location data is used to improve nearby activity discovery and safety features.

You may disable location permissions at any time through your device settings.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal data while your account is active.

You may delete your account at any time from within the app. Upon account deletion:

  • Your profile data will be permanently removed
  • Your personal information will be deleted from active systems
  • Some limited records may be retained if required for legal or safety purposes

7. Security

We use industry-standard security practices to protect user data. However, no system can guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

Dovabl is intended only for users aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app evolves during testing and development. Continued use of the app after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
